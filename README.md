🛡️ Zavala Sovereignty Protocol (ZSP) v2.1
Bridging Physical Operations and Strategic Reasoning
ZSP is a modular framework designed to map the "Behavioral Physics" of complex systems. By synthesizing real-world operational friction (The Warehouse) with structured legal heuristics (The Law Library), ZSP provides a high-fidelity audit of systemic health.

🧩 The Core Concept: The Bridge
Modern data analysis often ignores the "Friction" of the physical world. ZSP bridges this gap by categorizing data through three primary operational states:

Conductor: Optimal flow; low-resistance strategic alignment.

Insulator: Static or bureaucratic bottlenecks; potential for stagnation.

Corroded: Active systemic failure or high-resistance behavioral patterns.

🏗️ Modular Architecture
The ZSP engine is decoupled from industry specifics, allowing for "Plug-and-Play" domain application:

Copper Scout: Monitoring supply chain conductivity and mineral logistics.

Docket Health: Auditing legal filings for substantive "Brief" vs. "Boilerplate" value.

Compliance Audit: Identifying regulatory exposure through behavioral telemetry.
